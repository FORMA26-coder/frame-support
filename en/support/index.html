Support

Last updated: 24 November 2026

If something is not working, start here. If this page does not answer it, write to frame.app.11@gmail.com. The app is made by one person, so a reply may take a few days.

Questions people ask

Where are my photos and entries kept?

On your iPhone or iPad, and — if you have it turned on — in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see them.

Can I see the same records on my iPhone and iPad?

Yes, as long as both are signed in to the same Apple Account and Settings → iCloud is on in the app. The first sync can take a little while.

It says iCloud is unavailable

Check:

  1. iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud Drive is on
  2. FRAME. is allowed to use iCloud
  3. You have a network connection

Your records stay on the device while you are offline, and sync when you are back.

I'm changing phones

Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new device and install FRAME.; your records come back from iCloud. Check they are there before you delete the app from the old device.

I'm not getting notifications

Care reminders arrive only for tasks where you turned the reminder on. Also check iOS Settings → Notifications → FRAME.

I deleted an entry by mistake

Settings → Recently Deleted brings it back within 30 days.

My cat has died. What happens to our records?

Marking them In Memory keeps every entry and photo exactly as it is; only care reminders and everyday logging stop. Nothing is deleted.

If I cancel FRAME. Plus, do I lose what I made?

No. Only the Plus templates, widget looks and hiding the mark stop. Your diary, photos, care and memories are untouched.

Where do I cancel?

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → FRAME. Plus, any time.

I found a bug

Email frame.app.11@gmail.com with:

  • what you were doing (for example, saving an entry)
  • your device and iOS version
  • a screenshot, if you can

Please do not send your photos or diary text. We cannot read them and do not need them.
