Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

FRAME. is an app for keeping each day with your cat as a photo and a few words. It collects no personal data.

1. What the app holds

Inside the app there are:

  • your cat's name, birthday and the day they became family
  • diary entries and their dates
  • photos
  • care tasks (nail trims, medication, vet visits) and their history

These are stored on your iPhone or iPad, and — if you have iCloud turned on — in your own iCloud (the private database of Apple's CloudKit). The developer cannot read them.

2. Nothing is sent anywhere

The app sends no data to the developer's servers or to any third party.

  • No advertising
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA); you are not tracked
  • No account to create; no email address or phone number is asked for

The only network traffic the app makes is syncing with Apple's iCloud. That is your iCloud, not a copy sent to the developer.

3. Photos

Photos are read only when you pick them. Permission to add to your photo library is requested only when you save a picture made in FRAME Studio. Full access to your library is never requested.

4. Notifications

Care reminders ask for permission only when you turn one on, and are scheduled on your device alone. The developer never sends you a notification.

5. Purchases (FRAME. Plus)

Purchases of FRAME. Plus are handled by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

6. Children

The app is rated 4+. No personal information is collected from children — or from anyone.

7. Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes your records from the device and from iCloud. There is nothing held by the developer, so there is nothing to ask us to delete.

8. Changes to this policy

If analytics are ever added, this page will be revised before they are, and the App Store privacy labels will be updated at the same time.
